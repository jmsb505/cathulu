**Owner policy on escalation (2026-10-25, applies to both lanes).** This is a working team with the Owner in charge, not a policy of keeping the Owner out of the loop. A task that stops or fails goes up the hierarchy. Project Managers handle what they can; Workers report up. A blocker stays visible: anyone looking at the board can see what blocked the task and why. Items that need the Owner go to the Main Overseer, and the Overseer brings them to the Owner. Items that the team can resolve are handled among the agents without Owner interaction, and they are still visible. Escalation is about who acts, not about hiding information from the Owner. Serious failures reach the Owner directly through the Overseer.
